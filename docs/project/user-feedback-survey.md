# User Feedback Survey

**Version**: 1.0 · For use in: pilot interviews (5–10 participants)
**Format**: 10 questions, ~8 minutes to complete. Can be run as a live interview or sent as a written form (Typeform / Google Forms).
**Target**: freelancers, diaspora senders, or SME finance leads who send money internationally.

---

## Screening (confirm before starting)

> "In the last 12 months, have you sent money from Europe or North America to someone in Nigeria, India, or Brazil — either as a personal remittance or as a business payment to a contractor?"

- Yes → continue
- No → thank them and close

---

## Section A — Pain points (unprompted)

**Q1.** When you last sent money abroad, what bothered you most about the process? *(open-ended, max 2 minutes — let them speak before asking follow-ups)*

> Probe if not mentioned: speed / cost / hidden fees / knowing the money arrived / having to explain what service to use to the recipient?

---

**Q2.** On a scale of 1–5, how confident were you that the compliance checks (identity verification, source of funds) were actually done correctly?

```
1 — Not confident at all
2 — Slightly confident
3 — Neutral / didn't think about it
4 — Fairly confident
5 — Completely confident
```

*If 1–3:* "What would have made you more confident?"

---

## Section B — Concept reaction

*Read this aloud (or display it verbatim):*

> "Imagine a service where you pay by card in euros. A licensed financial partner converts the money to a regulated stablecoin, which settles to the recipient's bank account in seconds — they receive local currency, nothing crypto. At no point does either of you need a crypto wallet or know what a blockchain is. And every compliance check in the process is cryptographically timestamped on a public ledger, so a regulator — or you — can verify the checks actually happened, without relying on the company's own records."

---

**Q3.** What is your immediate reaction to this? *(open-ended)*

---

**Q4.** On a scale of 1–5, how likely would you be to try this for your next international transfer?

```
1 — Very unlikely
2 — Unlikely
3 — Neutral
4 — Likely
5 — Very likely
```

*If 1–3:* "What would need to change for you to try it?"

---

**Q5.** What concerns, if any, do you have about this service?

*(Prompt if silent: cost? safety of funds? whether it's legal? whether the recipient can access the money?)*

---

## Section C — Value proposition sharpening

**Q6.** The fact that compliance checks are verifiable on a public ledger — how valuable is that to you personally?

```
1 — Not valuable (I don't care about the audit trail)
2 — Slightly valuable
3 — Neutral / I hadn't thought about it
4 — Valuable — I'd feel more secure using the service
5 — Very valuable — this is a meaningful reason to choose this over alternatives
```

*If 4–5:* "Can you say more about why?" *(The answer here is your strongest marketing message.)*
*If 1–2:* "What would make you feel secure about the service instead?" *(note verbatim)*

---

**Q7.** What would you expect to pay in total fees for sending €500 to Nigeria / India / Brazil? *(accept their current expectation unprompted)*

> Interviewer note: this calibrates pricing tolerance. Compare against your current fee model (flat fee + stated FX spread).

---

## Section D — Trust & channels

**Q8.** How did you find your current money-transfer service? *(tick all that apply)*

- [ ] Recommendation from a friend or family member
- [ ] Community group (WhatsApp, forum, diaspora association)
- [ ] Comparison website (MoneySuperMarket, Monito, etc.)
- [ ] Social media / influencer
- [ ] Bank recommendation
- [ ] Just searched online
- [ ] Other: ___________

> Interviewer note: dominant channel = first acquisition channel to invest in.

---

**Q9.** If you learned that a new service was officially partnered with a local mobile money provider in the recipient's country (e.g. MTN MoMo in Nigeria, or Airtel Money in India), how much more likely would that make you to try it?

```
1 — No difference
2 — Slightly more likely
3 — Moderately more likely
4 — Much more likely
5 — That would be a key reason to switch
```

---

## Section E — Open close

**Q10.** Is there anything else about sending money abroad that really frustrates you — that you wish someone would solve?

*(Take verbatim notes. This is often where the most useful insight lives.)*

---

## Interviewer debrief (not shown to participant)

After each session, rate these three signals:

| Signal | Your assessment (1–5) |
|---|---|
| Pain intensity: how viscerally did they describe the problem? | |
| Concept pull: did they ask questions, or just answer? | |
| Willingness to pay: did their stated fee expectation match or exceed our model? | |

**Threshold to proceed**: if ≥ 6 of 10 participants score 4–5 on Q4, the core concept is validated. If Q6 scores consistently below 3, deprioritise the compliance-trail angle in consumer messaging and lead with speed + fee transparency instead.

---

## How to document results for the submission

1. Anonymise all participants (P1–P10, country, occupation only).
2. Record Q3, Q5, Q10 verbatim — quotes are evidence.
3. Tabulate Q4 and Q6 scores as a distribution.
4. Write a 2-paragraph summary: what the top pain point was, and whether participants valued the verifiable compliance trail or needed a different angle.
5. Commit the summary to `docs/analysis/testing-validation.md` under a new "External Validation" heading.
